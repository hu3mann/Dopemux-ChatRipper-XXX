# ChatX rearchitecture — deep-research targets

Auditor: **codex**. Date: **2026-10-20**. Companion: [audit report](report-codex.md). These **50 proposed targets** identify decisions to settle; they are not completed experiments or authorization to access personal data, install software, call models or change repositories.

Finding IDs refer to Output A. Phase names refer to the supplied design. **P0** means settle before the affected architecture or privacy boundary is accepted; **P1** means required for the relevant phase exit; **P2** means improve a qualified baseline; **P3** means exploratory. Effort is relative: **S** bounded document/fixture investigation; **M** comparative prototype or evaluation; **L** substantial corpus, measurement or cross-system study. These are scope estimates, not measured durations.

Every research return should identify its exact sources/artifacts, methods, versions and uncertainty; separate `CONFIRMED`, `PLAUSIBLE`, and `UNKNOWN`; retain negative results; and state the decision unlocked. Private-source studies require a separate authorized local workflow. Synthetic evidence must remain labelled synthetic.
