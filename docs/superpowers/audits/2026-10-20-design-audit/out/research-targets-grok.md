# ChatX rearchitecture — deep-research targets (grok)

Tied to the audit in `report-grok.md`. A good answer is a decision the builder can execute, with the source named and the measurement stated. Priorities: P0 blocks schema freeze or the privacy gate, P1 blocks the phase it names, P2 improves correctness or cost, P3 is useful and not on the critical path. Effort is the research, not the implementation: S under a day, M a few days, L a week or more including a real-db or model run.
